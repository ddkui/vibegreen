About Green Luzern

Discover the sustainable side of the city

Green Luzern is your dedicated interactive guide to finding eco-friendly, sustainable, and local businesses in and around Lucerne, Switzerland.

Whether you are looking for vegan dining, zero-waste shopping, eco-hotels, or practical stops like EV charging stations and bike rentals, our map makes it effortless to navigate the green best of the region.

Join us in promoting Swisstainable tourism and supporting local initiatives that make a positive impact on our environment.

🇨🇭 Swisstainable Program

Switzerland Tourism's sustainability program recognizes businesses committed to the environment. Look for these levels on our map:

I
Level I – committed: Organizations that commit to sustainable business management.
II
Level II – engaged: Organizations with a recognized sustainability certification.
III
Level III – leading: For organizations with a comprehensive sustainability certification.
