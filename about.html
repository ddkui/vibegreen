Our Mission

What is Green Luzern?

Green Luzern was created to make sustainable living in Lucerne effortless and accessible to everyone — from long-time locals to first-time visitors. We believe that knowing where to find eco-friendly options is the first step towards a more sustainable daily life.

Our interactive map aggregates carefully researched, real-world sustainable locations — from zero-waste grocery stores and vegan restaurants to EV charging stations and certified Swisstainable businesses — all in one beautifully designed platform.

We take inspiration from the Swisstainable movement led by Switzerland Tourism, which promotes tourism and daily life that strengthens the local economy, protects natural landscapes, and respects cultural heritage.

Our Values

Why we built this

Community First

Every location on our map is a real, locally-operating business contributing to a more sustainable Lucerne.

Privacy Respecting

We collect no personal data and use your location only on-device for routing. Your privacy is not a trade-off.

Open Technology

Built on OpenStreetMap and open-source routing, we support the infrastructure that powers transparent, accessible mapping.

Always Growing

We continuously add new green locations across all categories to keep the map relevant and useful for you.

Our Inspiration

Proud to support Swisstainable

The Swisstainable programme is Switzerland Tourism's official framework recognising businesses that commit to responsible, sustainable practices. Green Luzern proudly aligns with these values and highlights certified local places across Lucerne.
