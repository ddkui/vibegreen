Our Mission

What is Green Luzern?

Green Luzern helps locals and visitors find places and practical stops in and around Luzern: where to eat, shop secondhand, refill a bottle, or rent a bike.

The map brings together food, shopping, places to stay, cultural venues, bike rentals, charging points, and public drinking fountains. Check current details with the venue before visiting.

We take inspiration from the Swisstainable movement led by Switzerland Tourism, which promotes tourism and daily life that strengthens the local economy, protects natural landscapes, and respects cultural heritage.

Categories

What you can find

Green Luzern covers 9 categories of sustainable locations across the Lucerne region:

Plant-based food

Vegan and vegetarian venues, plus places with plant-based options.

Refill & groceries

Packaging-free shops and bulk food stores.

Places to stay

Hotels with sustainability initiatives listed in the guide.

Culture & outdoors

Cultural venues, outdoor activities, and local meeting places.

Second Hand

Vintage and pre-loved fashion stores.

EV Charging

Public electric vehicle charging stations.

Bike Rental

Nextbike and city bike-sharing stations.

Swisstainable

Businesses listed under Switzerland Tourism’s sustainability programme.

Drinking Water

Public fountains for refilling your bottle.

Our Values

Why we built this

Community First

Every location on our map is a real, locally-operating business contributing to a more sustainable Lucerne.

Privacy Respecting

You can browse without an account. Location access is optional. See our privacy policy for how maps, search, and routing services handle requests.

Open Technology

Built on OpenStreetMap and open-source routing, we support the infrastructure that powers transparent, accessible mapping.

Always Growing

We continuously add new green locations across all categories to keep the map relevant and useful for you.

Our Inspiration

Proud to support Swisstainable

The Swisstainable programme is Switzerland Tourism's official framework recognising businesses that commit to responsible, sustainable practices. Green Luzern proudly aligns with these values and highlights certified local places across Lucerne.
