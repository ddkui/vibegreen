# Listing review — 3 October 2026

All 75 legacy records were assessed against a publication threshold: an identifiable venue or facility, documented inclusion basis, and usable placement. Twenty existing records remain, with corrected addresses, descriptions and pins where needed. Twelve new records bring the public map to 32 listings across all nine categories. Fifty-five legacy entries are withheld pending evidence; their identifiers and decisions are preserved in `location-review.json`. Withholding is not a finding that a venue is closed or unsustainable.

## Inclusion policy

Each public record includes a concrete reason, primary operator/municipal/programme sources (or the local tourism authority), and a source-review date. Plant-based menus, refill services, secondhand resale, repair, cycle rental and public charging qualify as specific services; they do not certify the whole business or establish net-zero emissions. Organic retail is distinguished from packaging-free shopping. EV availability and opening hours remain the operator's responsibility.

Swisstainable is a programme, not a new standalone certification. Level I records are labelled committed, Level II engaged and Level III leading. Level I is not described as independently certified. The [programme guidance](https://www.stv-fst.ch/nachhaltigkeit/kompetenzzentrum/swisstainable) and [participant register](https://st.stnet.ch/nachhaltigkeit/pages/public/service_provider.jsf) support these distinctions. SDG links are educational associations, not UN endorsements.

Commercial pins use the federal [GeoAdmin address search](https://docs.geo.admin.ch/access-data/search.html) and matched building addresses. The Velostation address is indexed as the combined Frohburgstrasse 3.5 building. Stadtkeller's pin marks Sternenplatz near its stated venue address; the card states this approximation. Three public fountains use square-level pins and the city's [fountain guide](https://www.stadtluzern.ch/_docn/5020288/neu_Brunnenbroschuere_2022_2Aufl2024_WEB.pdf). These are not surveyed entrance positions. Fuzzy geocoder results on a different street were rejected; the candidate Fruttstrasse charger was not published.

- The Kriens cableway pin is correctly named the valley station, rather than Krienseregg.
- Event-based Repair Café and Preloved Pop-up records explicitly require checking dates. The festival pin denotes its KKL venue.
- Unsupported blanket climate-neutral, certified, regional-food and refill claims are removed.
